# Model Card: PortWatch AI Congestion Forecaster

## Model Details
- **Developed by:** PortWatch AI Team

- **Model type:** XGBoost Regressor + Classifier ensemble
- **Version:** 1.0.0
- **License:** MIT

## Intended Use
- Forecast port congestion 7–14 days ahead for research and educational purposes
- Identify anomaly patterns in maritime trade flows

## Ethical Considerations
- Higher-income ports have richer data; model may underperform for developing economies
- Predictions should not replace human operational judgment
